## Realistic request fit

For a reactive client status label, the guidance gives a coherent implementation boundary: a client feature lifecycle root imports `ReplicatedStorage.Packages.Fusion`, creates one typed `Fusion.Scope<typeof(Fusion)>`, stores UI-local status in a scoped `Value`, derives label text with `Computed`, and observes future changes with a scoped `Observer`. `scope.OnEvent(...)` makes the UI event connection scope-owned. The feature owner registers the producer unsubscribe and `Fusion.doCleanup(scope)` with its Janitor; a child component accepts the borrowed scope and does not clean it. Construction belongs in protected error handling so an already-created scope is cleaned after a body failure while preserving the original body error separately from a cleanup error.

The maintained fixture exercises those mechanics with deterministic changes: it checks initial and reactive `TextLabel.Text`, observes a derived value, fires a scope-owned `BindableEvent`, injects a construction failure into a second scope, checks failed-scope cleanup, cleans the main scope, compares the parent's final child count to its baseline, and captures Fusion warnings/errors.

A matching pin plus an unblocked schema-v3 record permits ordinary version-sensitive use after the cheap installed-state checks. A safe, reversible workaround that is likely to recur is a soft defect: immediate work may continue, but the reproduction, workaround, and durable correction must enter repair. A defect that makes correctness, identity, security, or verification unreliable is hard: stop dependent claims and repair before proceeding.
